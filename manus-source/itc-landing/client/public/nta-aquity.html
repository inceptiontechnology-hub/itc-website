How it works

A screen wide enough to catch what you didn't know to look for.

Non-targeted analysis (NTA) doesn't require a predefined compound list. It sweeps across the full organic chemical landscape — detecting, identifying, and ranking 72,000+ compounds from a single 25 mL water sample.

Those results feed into the Aquity platform, which cross-references detected compounds against industrial and geographic data to identify likely source industries — and name specific facilities. Operators stop chasing unknowns and start taking action.

Where conventional labs tell you what was found, Aquity tells you where it came from and why it matters for treatment operations.

How this works

Analysis is performed by CEC Analytics at their Calgary, AB facility. Inception Technology Company is a U.S. distributor — we connect municipalities, utilities, and industry with the technology and manage deployment. ITC does not operate the instruments or run the analysis itself.

What Aquity identifies

Compound categories and their treatment implications.

Biocides

Disinfectants & antimicrobials

Highest effluent risk category. Persists through treatment and interferes with biological processes.

Foamants & emulsifiers

Surfactant-class compounds

Linked to foaming events and nitrification inhibition. Traceable to specific industrial dischargers via trunk line sampling.

PFAS & fluorinated

Regulated & emerging fluorinated compounds

Tracked with EPA DTXSID registry linkage. Regulatory alerts flagged for high-concern categories.

Pharmaceuticals

Human & veterinary medications

Fingerprinted by drug class with both human and veterinary compound identification.

Agricultural runoff

Herbicides, pesticides & transformation products

Agricultural input compounds and their breakdown products, with seasonal trend capability.

Industrial & structural

Flame retardants, crosslinkers & stabilizers

Compounds from construction, electronics, textiles, and plastics manufacturing — ranked by exposure and severity.
