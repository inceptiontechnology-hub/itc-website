You can’t test for what you don’t know is there.

Traditional targeted testing starts with a question: “Is compound X present?” That works when you already know what to look for. It is much less useful when you are trying to explain unexplained foaming, toxicity, COD burden, nitrification problems, a WET failure, or an unknown industrial discharge.
