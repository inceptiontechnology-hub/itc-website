Meet Aquity

Thousands of detections are only useful if you can understand them.

Aquity is your portal to all of your NTA sample data. It organizes, analyzes, and tracks over time. Rather than handing you a wall of compounds, you receive a complex overview of your test results, organized so you can clearly see patterns and findings that deserve attention with ease.

  • Prioritize high-severity findings and categories.
  • Explore industrial, agricultural, pharmaceutical and fluorinated compound signals.
  • Connect chemical findings to potential sources and vulnerable processes.
  • Turn complex analytical output into something your team can actually explore.
