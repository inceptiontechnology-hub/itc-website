How it works

A bacterial test that works in anyone's hands.

The Rapid Bacterial Test requires 3 mL of water, returns results in 10 seconds, and requires zero technical training to operate. Detection is built into the test itself — no interpretation, no equipment, no expertise required.

This isn't a simplified version of a lab test. It's a purpose-built field detection tool designed for the real world — where speed and simplicity determine whether testing happens at all.

Current market leaders — 3M, Thermo Fisher, Danaher — dominate a $2.3B space with solutions that require laboratories, trained personnel, and significant time. The Rapid Bacterial Test competes on every dimension that matters to operators in the field.

How this works

The Rapid Bacterial Test is developed and produced by CEC Analytics. Inception Technology Company is a U.S. distributor — we connect municipalities, utilities, NGOs, and industry with the technology and manage deployment in the United States.
