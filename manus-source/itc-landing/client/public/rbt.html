How it works

A bacterial test that works in anyone's hands.

The Rapid Bacterial Test requires 3 mL of water, returns results in 10 seconds, and requires zero technical training to operate. Detection is built into the test itself. No equipment or expertise is required.

This isn't a simplified version of a lab test. It's a purpose-built field detection tool designed for the real world, where speed and simplicity determine whether testing happens at all.

Conventional solutions often require laboratories, trained personnel, and significant time. The Rapid Bacterial Test is designed around the speed and simplicity operators need in the field.
