What is AIMS?

Targeted detection at the parts-per-trillion level.

AIMS uses ambient ionization mass spectrometry to identify and quantify defined panels of known compounds in water, wastewater, and biological samples.

It is built for speed and precision when the target is already known. Detection limits reach 5 ppt for drugs and most compounds and approximately 10 ppt for some PFAS species, with rapid results issued shortly after sample receipt at CEC’s Calgary laboratory.

Conventional LC-MS and GC-MS workflows typically require one to two weeks.

Choosing the right analysis

NTA or AIMS?

Use NTA + Aquity when you need to discover what is present and investigate where it came from.

Use AIMS when you already know what you need to measure and want fast, quantitative results.

The two approaches can also work together: broad discovery followed by focused measurement.
