How AIMS works

Targeted detection at the parts-per-trillion level.

AIMS (Ambient Ionization Mass Spectrometry) uses targeted analysis to identify and quantify specific panels of known compounds in a water, wastewater, or biological sample. Unlike non-targeted screening, AIMS is built for speed and precision on a defined compound list.

Detection limits reach 5 ppt (parts per trillion) for drugs and most compounds — roughly 10 ppt for some PFAS species. Results are returned within 48 hours of sample receipt at CEC's Calgary laboratory.

Conventional LC-MS/GC-MS labs typically require 1–2 weeks for the same analysis — and do not offer the rapid deployment AIMS provides.

How this works

Analysis is performed by CEC Analytics at their Calgary, AB facility. Inception Technology Company is a U.S. distributor — we connect municipalities, utilities, and industry with the technology and manage deployment. ITC does not operate the instruments or run the analysis itself.

The panels

Five targeted panels available today.

SoPA — Substances of Potential Abuse

The most comprehensive targeted drug panel available. Covers fentanyl and its analogues (including carfentanil, alfentanil, sufentanil, remifentanil), opiates (morphine, codeine, hydromorphone), amphetamines (methamphetamine, MDMA, MDA), benzodiazepines (diazepam, alprazolam, clonazepam, lorazepam, etizolam, and more), cocaine and metabolites, methadone/EDDP, buprenorphine, naloxone, psilocybin/psilocin, ketamine, LSD, PCP — plus emerging substances including xylazine, levamisole, gabapentin, and alcohol/nicotine biomarkers.

Fluorinated compounds

PFAS — Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances

Targeted quantification of PFOS and PFOA. Detection approximately 10 ppt for some PFAS species due to matrix complexity.

Industrial solvent

Sulfolane

Targeted detection and quantification of sulfolane — a soil and groundwater contaminant associated with natural gas sweetening and refinery operations.

Herbicide

Glyphosate

Targeted detection and quantification of glyphosate in water and environmental matrices.

Hormone

Progesterone

Targeted detection and quantification of progesterone — relevant for agricultural runoff and endocrine disruption monitoring.
