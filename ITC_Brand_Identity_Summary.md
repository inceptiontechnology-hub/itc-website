## Mission

**Regenerate Earth's biosphere.**

Every product, system, and connection should be evaluated against this mission. ITC builds a portfolio of technologies that are both *economically superior* and *ecologically regenerative* — these are not trade-offs; they are requirements.

## Brand Voice

- **Direct and clear.** No fluff.
- **Professional but not stiff.**
- **Action-oriented.** Come back with answers, not questions.
- **Grounded in lived experience.** Scott *was* the customer — farmer, cattle operator, wastewater professional. That shapes how ITC speaks.
- **Guiding principle (Scott's rule):** *"Be brief, be bright, and be gone."*

**Hero copy from the live website:**
> *"There is a kind of person who sees what this century will demand. They do not think the work is too hard. They do not think the odds are too long. They do not think someone else will get to it in time.*
> *ITC is the company built for them. And by them."*

## Visual Identity

### Design Concept: "Cosmic Regeneration"
The chosen website design direction is **Futuristic Minimalism / Space Age** — the Earth as protagonist, technology as steward.

### Color Palette
| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Deep Space Black | `#000000` |
| Primary / Earth | Luminous Earth Blue | `#2E86AB` |
| Header Text | Starlight White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Accent (life/regeneration) | Regeneration Green | `#4CAF50` |
| Highlight / Active | Cyan | `#64FFDA` |

*CSS uses oklch color space; primary is `oklch(0.55 0.15 240)` (luminous blue).*

### Typography
| Use | Font | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Logo / Primary headers | **Orbitron** | Geometric sans-serif, bold, all-caps, futuristic |
| Subheadings / Body | **Rajdhani** | Clean, technical, tracking-wide |

### Design Language
- **3D animated Earth globe** as the homepage hero (Three.js / React Three Fiber)
- **Starfield background** with depth (parallax)
- **Floating elements** — text suspended in space, not boxed in
- **Glassmorphism panels** — semi-transparent dark glass for nav and text areas
- **Thin-line iconography** — precise, HUD-style
- **Slow, deliberate animations** — long fade-ins, subtle glows, smooth transitions
- Rounded nav with `hover:text-primary` transitions

## Brand Positioning

ITC is not a traditional distributor or startup. It sits at the intersection of:
- **Deep domain knowledge** (farming, wastewater, cattle, ecology, public health)
- **Cutting-edge technology** (compound analysis, rumen buffering, biogenic chemistry)
- **Regenerative mission** (the tech must do both — economic and ecological)

The brand should feel like it was built by people who have *done the work*, not people who read about it. Earned credibility, not borrowed.
