How it works

Lab-grade bacterial testing, in the field.

The kit needs just 3 mL of water and returns a result in about ten seconds. It is a colorimetric presence/absence test, read by eye against the key below — no instrument to run and nothing to calibrate. That moves bacterial testing out of the lab and into the hands of the people who need the answer.

01

Ten-second results

03

A clear yes or no
