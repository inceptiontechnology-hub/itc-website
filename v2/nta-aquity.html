How it works

A wide net, then a clear picture.

Traditional water testing only detects the compounds it is specifically programmed to find. Non-targeted analysis (NTA) uses advanced mass spectrometry to scan a single 25 mL grab sample for more than 72,000 organic compounds at once — with no pre-programmed target list. It works across water, wastewater, biosolids, foam, FOG, and storm water.

Results are then processed through CEC’s proprietary Aquity platform, which turns raw chemistry into clarity, context, and action — not just a list of compounds, but what they mean for your treatment operations.

The difference

Labs using LC-MS / GC-MS can match the 1–2 week turnaround — but they hand back raw compound data. Aquity is the only platform that identifies contamination sources by geography and industry, turning chemistry into decisions.

What Aquity shows you

Chemistry, translated into operations.

01

Compound intelligence

Classifies every detected compound by function — biocides, flame retardants, foamants, PFAS, pharmaceuticals, agricultural runoff — and links each to treatment risk.

02

Source attribution

Identifies which industries, and often which specific facilities, are discharging problem compounds — ranked by volume and severity.

03

Process vulnerability ranking

Scores every treatment process — nitrification, anaerobic digestion, clarification, membranes — by its exposure to detected compounds.

04

Removal efficiency

Tracks what your plant removes versus what passes through to effluent, broken down by compound class.

05

THOD analysis

Quantifies oxygen-demand load split between residential and industrial sources, with time-series trend tracking.

06

PFAS & regulatory tracking

Flags fluorinated compounds with EPA DTXSID registry links for compliance reporting.

07

High-severity alerts

Real-time alerts for critical compound categories, with signal strength and risk classification.
